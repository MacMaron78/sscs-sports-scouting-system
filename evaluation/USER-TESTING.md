### Scoring

Calculate a score only when all ten responses are present:

1. For odd-numbered items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9), subtract 1 from the response.
2. For even-numbered items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10), subtract the response from 5.
3. Add the ten adjusted values and multiply the total by 2.5. The result is the participant's SUS score, from 0 to 100.

The SUS score is not a percentage. Report individual scores and descriptive summary statistics, such as the mean and range, alongside the participant count. Do not claim that a score proves the system is usable on its own; discuss it with task completion, observed issues, and participant comments.
