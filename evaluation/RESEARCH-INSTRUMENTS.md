# SSCS Research Data-Collection Instruments

These are working drafts aligned with the proposal's interviews, athlete questionnaire, and document analysis. They do not replace institutional ethics review, an approved consent process, or any required permission to contact participants. Use only after the supervisor/institution approves the instruments. Do not fill in responses on behalf of participants.

## Participant information and consent checklist

Before each interview, questionnaire, or usability session:

- Explain the study purpose, what participation involves, and the expected duration.
- Explain that participation is voluntary and that the participant may decline a question or stop.
- Explain what information will be recorded, who can access it, how it will be stored, and when it will be deleted under the approved retention policy.
- Explain whether notes, audio, screenshots, or recordings will be made; obtain separate permission where required.
- Explain how anonymous codes will be used in reports and presentations.
- Obtain and record consent using the institution-approved form before collecting data.
- For participants under 18, follow institutional requirements for assent and parent/guardian permission. Do not recruit minors until the required approval and safeguards are in place.

This checklist is not itself an approved consent form. Attach the approved information sheet and consent form to the final report where required.

## A. Semi-structured interview guide

Use the same core questions with athletes and agency/scout representatives, adapting role-specific wording. Ask follow-up questions neutrally; do not suggest preferred answers.

### Opening and background

1. What is your role or involvement in sport?
2. How long have you been involved?
3. Which sport or sports do you work with or participate in?

### Current discovery and coordination

4. How do athletes and agencies/scouts currently find or contact one another?
5. How is athlete or scouting information currently recorded and organized?
6. What difficulties arise when looking for athletes or trying to gain exposure?
7. How do athletes and agencies/scouts communicate during this process?

### Platform needs and concerns

8. What information should an athlete profile contain?
9. Which filters would be useful when searching for athletes, and why?
10. Which platform functions would be most useful to you?
11. What supporting evidence (for example, statistics, career history, or an external video link) would be useful?
12. What concerns would you have about sharing or viewing information on a digital platform?
13. What access, privacy, or account-control features would you expect?

### Access and closing

14. What device and internet access do you normally have when using web services?
15. Have you used a similar platform? What worked well or poorly?
16. Is there anything important we have not discussed?

## B. Athlete questionnaire draft

Use an institution-approved survey tool and settings. Do not collect names, email addresses, or account credentials unless explicitly required and approved. Make questions optional where the proposal says responses are anonymous and optional. Pilot the form before distribution.

1. **Age range:** Under 18 / 18-24 / 25-30 / Over 30 / Prefer not to say.
2. **Sport(s) played:** Free text.
3. **Highest level played:** Club / Regional / National / International / Other / Prefer not to say.
4. **Which devices can you access?** Smartphone / Tablet / Laptop or desktop / Other / None.
5. **How often can you access the internet?** Daily / Weekly / Occasionally / Rarely / Never.
6. **Have you ever been contacted by a scout or sports agency?** Yes / No / Prefer not to say.
7. **If yes, how were you first contacted?** In person / Social media / Referral / Website or platform / Other / Prefer not to say.
8. **How satisfied were you with your discovery or representation experience?** 1 Very dissatisfied / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 Very satisfied / Not applicable.
9. **How important would each feature be to you?** Rate 1 Not important to 5 Very important:
   - A profile with a biography and sport-specific performance information.
   - Adding a link to an externally hosted performance video.
   - Direct messaging with agencies or scouts.
   - Search filters such as sport, position/event, age, and location.
   - A notification when an agency or scout contacts you.
   - A way to show verified sporting credentials, if supported in a future version.
10. **What would make you comfortable or uncomfortable using a digital scouting platform?** Optional free text.
11. **What feature or improvement would be most useful to you?** Optional free text.

The proposal mentions a five-point feature-importance scale. Do not combine feature-importance responses with satisfaction or usability scores; report each question according to its scale. Describe the actual sample and limitations, including the small purposive sample.
